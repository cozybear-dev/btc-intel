# O-WEAK-KEY-SWEEP — Weak key-generation exploitation

> **Tactic:** Acquisition · **OAK:** — · **FATF red-flag areas:** source_of_funds

## Summary

Keys generated with too little entropy are brute-forced offline and swept, often years later and within minutes across many addresses (LuBian 2020, Milk Sad 2023, Coldcard Mk3 2026).

Examples: LuBian 2020, Milk Sad (libbitcoin bx) 2023, Coldcard Mk3 firmware 2026.

## How it works

```
 weak RNG seeds ─► attacker enumerates keys offline ─► sweeps every funded match within minutes
```
- **Milk Sad (2023)**: Libbitcoin Explorer 3.x derived `bx seed` entropy from a Mersenne Twister seeded with 32 bits of system time; the researchers confirmed use in over 2,600 mainnet wallets and linked it to about $900k of thefts [milksad-2023].
- **LuBian (2020)**: Arkham reports that more than 90% of the pool's BTC was drained on 28 December 2020 and attributes it to weak private-key generation; the theft went unnoticed for nearly five years [arkham-lubian-2025].
- **Coldcard Mk3 (2026)**: a firmware flaw weakened seed randomness; about 1,082 BTC was swept from about 1,196 single-signature addresses in 41 minutes, many of them dormant for years [coindesk-coldcard-2026].

## Signals and thresholds

- Bursts of sweeps from unrelated, dormant single-sig addresses into few destinations ([H-MOTIF-SWEEP](../heuristics/h-motif-sweep.md), [H-TEMPORAL](../heuristics/h-temporal.md)).

Detected by: [H-MOTIF-SWEEP](../heuristics/h-motif-sweep.md), [H-TEMPORAL](../heuristics/h-temporal.md). Evades: —.

## False positives and limitations

- Owners migrating many wallets at once after a security advisory.

## Evasion / countermeasures

Hardware or audited entropy sources; move funds away from any wallet generated by affected software or firmware.

## Detection in btc-intel

Planned (H-MOTIF-SWEEP needs prevout ages and timing windows). Today an analyst can trace the sweep destinations forward from any sweep transaction.
