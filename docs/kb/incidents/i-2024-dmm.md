# I-2024-DMM — DMM Bitcoin hack

> **Date:** 2024-05-31 · **Loss:** 4,502.9 BTC (~$305M) · **Attribution:** confirmed (North Korea's TraderTraitor / Lazarus Group)

## Summary

On 31 May 2024 DMM Bitcoin lost 4,502.9 BTC (about $305M) in one transaction to `1B6rJRfjTXwEy36SCs5zofGMmdv2kdZw7P`, which split the coins into ten wallets of 500 BTC [rekt-dmm-2024]. U.S. and Japanese authorities attributed the hack to North Korea in December 2024 [coindesk-dmm-attribution-2024].

## How it works

[O-KEY-COMPROMISE](../techniques/o-key-compromise.md): DMM did not disclose the technical cause; attribution rests on laundering similarities and off-chain indicators [cybernews-dmm-2024][coindesk-dmm-attribution-2024].

## Laundering timeline

- 31 May 2024: theft transaction `975ec405…e4d7` (4,502.9 BTC) and a split into ten 500-BTC wallets [rekt-dmm-2024].
- June–July 2024: the proceeds reportedly pass through a mixer ([O-CUSTODIAL-MIXER](../techniques/o-custodial-mixer.md)), are bridged via THORChain, Avalanche Bridge and Threshold ([O-CHAIN-HOP](../techniques/o-chain-hop.md)), swapped to USDT and moved to Tron [invezz-dmm-huione-2024].
- July 2024: about $35M reaches Huione Guarantee ([O-OTC-P2P](../techniques/o-otc-p2p.md)) [invezz-dmm-huione-2024].

Technique chain: [O-KEY-COMPROMISE](../techniques/o-key-compromise.md) (Key or signing-infrastructure compromise) → [O-CUSTODIAL-MIXER](../techniques/o-custodial-mixer.md) (Custodial mixers / tumblers) → [O-CHAIN-HOP](../techniques/o-chain-hop.md) (Cross-chain bridges and swap protocols) → [O-OTC-P2P](../techniques/o-otc-p2p.md) (OTC brokers, P2P markets and guarantee marketplaces).

## Signals and thresholds

- One large theft transaction, then a uniform split ([H-MOTIF-FANOUT](../heuristics/h-motif-fanout.md) with uniform values) and bridge memos ([H-XCHAIN](../heuristics/h-xchain.md)).

Trace forward from the theft transaction: `btcintel trace 975ec405ac9dc9fa5ab8009d94d6a1fe31dff8a8127ea90d023104e52754e4d7 --hops 4 --max-calls 400`; THORChain memos on the path reveal destination chains.

**Dogfood traces (2026-09-26).** Run with `--hops 4 --max-calls 300` against mempool.space, with tags imported from mining pools, OFAC, GraphSense and ransomwhe.re. Taint is haircut unless noted.

- Forward from the theft transaction (14 transactions, complete): the exploiter address `1B6rJRfjTXwEy36SCs5zofGMmdv2kdZw7P` pays out 500 BTC at a time and returns the change to itself ([H-CHG-SELF](../heuristics/h-chg-self.md)), so after four hops it still holds 2,502.9 BTC of taint (4,502.9 − 4 × 500), consistent with the reported split into 500-BTC wallets.
- Forward from the exploiter address (`btcintel trace 1B6rJRfjTXwEy36SCs5zofGMmdv2kdZw7P --hops 4 --max-calls 300`) accounts for 4,502.88 BTC across its stops, the theft minus fees. Every output paying the address, including each self-change output (4,002.9 → 3,502.9 → … → 502.9 BTC), starts at hop 1, so the 500-BTC payouts are followed further and split again into 250–300 BTC pieces by hop 4. The split-wallet seed `bc1qx6jpnnfjrfcx9ehhdmj7qqyzpyd8pek00trrq7` is one of the 500-BTC payouts.
- The first run of this trace reported 21,364 BTC because the engine counted the self-change outputs twice (as seeds and again as change); address seeds now count each output once.
- Forward from that split wallet shows further splitting (249.9996, 129.9992, 90.1 and 29.9 BTC at hop 4).
- Within four hops there is no THORChain memo and no labeled exit: the mixer and THORChain stages came weeks later, beyond this hop budget.
