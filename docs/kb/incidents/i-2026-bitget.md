# I-2026-BITGET — Bitget hot-wallet breach

> **Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Loss:** ~$351.6M across hot wallets · **Attribution:** suspected (Suspected North Korea / Lazarus (unconfirmed))

## Summary

At 18:31 UTC on 24 September 2026 Bitget detected unauthorised transfers from several hot wallets totalling about $351.6M; withdrawals were suspended and receiving addresses flagged [hackread-bitget-2026]. An on-chain investigator attributed the hack to Lazarus, but as of 25 September 2026 attribution remains **unconfirmed**.

## How it works

[O-KEY-COMPROMISE](../techniques/o-key-compromise.md) of part of the exchange's hot-wallet infrastructure; the exchange says cold wallets were untouched [hackread-bitget-2026].
